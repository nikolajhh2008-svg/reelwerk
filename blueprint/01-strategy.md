# 1 · Strategy – confirm, then fix the week

The analysis (00-analyze.md) proposes; the human decides. This step is one short conversation, then a file: `brand/strategy.md`. Repeat it every 30 days or when the numbers say so (08-learn.md).

## 1.1 One round of questions

Show the human a summary of `brand/project.md` (product in one sentence, two-second moments, how the product appears, proposed content families and buckets with shares) and ask **only what the analysis could not answer**, in one message. Voice answers are fine; messy is fine. Typical gaps:

1. **The one viewer.** Describe one real person who should watch: age, situation, what they want, what annoys them (Kallaway's "chair test": who sits across from you?). Not a group – one person.
2. **The goal.** What should a good video lead to: sign-ups, downloads, sales, a waitlist, followers? Pick one main measure.
3. **The mix.** Do the proposed families and buckets feel right? Anything that must never be done (no face, no voice, no jokes about X)?
4. **Raw material.** Is there a person behind the project with a story worth telling? Will they dictate a few minutes a week?
5. **Capacity.** How many videos per week can they watch and post (3–7 is realistic)?

## 1.2 The bullseye

Draw five rings from narrow to broad around the one viewer:

1. exactly the one viewer, today's problem,
2. everyone with that problem,
3. everyone in that situation,
4. the wider interest around it,
5. ⛔ the broad, generic interest (too broad: a viral hit here brings the wrong audience and confuses the algorithm about who to show you to).

Rings 1–3 bring the right people; ring 4 brings reach. Ring 5 is off-limits.

## 1.3 The weekly mix

Kallaway's 3-2-1 pattern for 7 videos, adapted to the buckets from the analysis:
- **1 broad bucket × 2** (ring 4: reach that still points at the right people),
- **2 narrow buckets × 2** (rings 2–3: the people you want),
- **1 wild card** (any ring, any family – a deliberate experiment).

Scale down proportionally for 3–5 videos. The family shares from 00-analyze.md (inform / entertain / show) apply across the week.

## 1.4 Formats this project can do

Mark which template formats in `studio/` are possible with the assets that exist (00-analyze.md, 0.4):

| Format | Needs |
|---|---|
| **Carousel** – 4–8 slides, swipe | any; best for inform (saves) |
| **Screen demo** – product moment with camera moves | option A, B or C |
| **Character skit** – mascot/character reacts | a character with several poses |
| **Comment reply** – a real comment on screen, answered | a real comment |
| **Countdown / series** – "day 12 of 100" | a real date or series |

Never two identical formats in a row.

## 1.5 Write `brand/strategy.md`

The one viewer · goal and main measure · bullseye · buckets with family and share · formats allowed · weekly count · posting days · what is off-limits. Then go to [02-ideas.md](02-ideas.md).
