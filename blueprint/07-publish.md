# 7 · Publish – by hand

Skills: `viral-captions-and-ctas`, `ig-caption` (caption text), `ig-plan`, `viral-tiktok-content`, `viral-instagram-reels` (timing and platform habits).

## Why by hand

- **TikTok:** an app that only posts to your own account does not pass TikTok's audit – "A utility tool to help upload contents to the account(s) you or your team manages" is listed as not acceptable – and unaudited apps can only post privately ([TikTok Content Sharing Guidelines](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-sharing-guidelines)). Post in the app, or through an audited scheduling tool.
- **Music is already in the video** (04-script.md, 4.6). Post with the original sound and add no app music on top. Only if a trend sound is the point of the video: use the app library (business accounts: the commercial library only), keep the original sound at 100 %, the added song at about 30–50 %, and set the slider last – it resets when the song changes.
- **Carousels:** TikTok photo mode has music only; on Instagram, adding music mutes a video's own sound inside a carousel.

## Platform rules that matter for coded videos

- TikTok counts "slideshow-only videos" as low quality in its creator rewards FAQ ([TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/creator-rewards-program)); Instagram shows "reels that are majority text" less ([Instagram](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained/)). Templates are fine; every video needs its own idea and more than text on slides.
- Repetitive, automated content counts as spam on TikTok ([TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity)). Rotate formats; never post the same video twice.
- AI labels: required for realistic AI people or scenes; not for drawn characters or real product recordings ([TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/ai-generated-content-label)). Don't use realistic AI people.
- Promoting your own product from a business account: switch on the platform's commercial-content / brand disclosure.

## Checklist per post

Caption pasted · original sound kept (no extra app music unless planned) · disclosure set · link tracked per video (e.g. `?ref=<id>`) · `work/videos/<id>/posted.md` with date, platform and link.
