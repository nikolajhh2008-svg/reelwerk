# 8 · Learn – real numbers decide

## Every week

1. The human drops analytics screenshots (TikTok Studio, Instagram Insights) into `work/results/<week>/`. The AI reads them: views, **3-second hold rate** (aim ≥ 70 %, under 50 % means the hook failed), average watch time, shares, saves, profile visits – and the project's own main measure from `brand/strategy.md` (sign-ups per video via the tracked link).
2. Break the top 3 into their parts: topic, angle, hook type, story shape, format, picture, sound. Make variants of winners: same structure, new topic ("six of seven bricks proven, one new").
3. Re-weight buckets, families (inform / entertain / show), formats and hook types in `brand/strategy.md` – write down why.
4. Add winning and losing hooks with their numbers to `work/taste/`; the next idea and hook runs read them.

## Every month

- **Test the judge:** take pairs of real videos ("A beat B") and check whether the ranking in 02-ideas.md and 03-hooks.md would have put them in the right order. If it is not clearly better than a coin flip, treat it as a pre-filter only and rely on the human more.
- Re-run [01-strategy.md](01-strategy.md); re-run [00-analyze.md](00-analyze.md) if the product changed.
- Check account health: TikTok "not eligible for For You" notices, Instagram account status.

With a real retention graph (screenshot from the platform's analytics), use `retention-audit` to map each drop to a beat of the storyboard – and record which shape (`work/videos/shapes.jsonl`) the winners had.
